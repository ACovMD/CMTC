Why this work

Two kinds of expertise that rarely sit in the same room.

Clinical fluency

An understanding of what a workflow actually needs to accomplish — what information a physician must get from a chart, an image, or a monitor in the middle of a shift, and how quickly.

Lived experience of the gap

Firsthand knowledge of exactly where accessible technology succeeds and where it quietly fails a working clinician — the difference between a tool that looks accessible and one that actually is.
